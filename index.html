Past projects

Built by students at AWS Hacks 2026.

Kural

A communication device for people with ALS. Eye tracking selects an intent, Amazon Bedrock writes the sentence, and it plays back in a voice cloned from the person’s own recordings.

Amazon BedrockSageMakerLambdaAPI GatewayDynamoDBS3Cognito

ARIA

Autonomous Response Incident Assistant. During a live 911 call it gathers incident details, unit availability, hospital capacity and protocols, then surfaces one recommendation for the dispatcher to approve.

Amazon BedrockPythonTypeScript

FishFlow

Upload a fish-passage design and a target species. FishFlow runs a CFD simulation on AWS, checks it against USFWS criteria, and returns a PDF verdict in about 30 seconds.

LambdaFargateAPI GatewayS3CloudFrontCognitoAmazon Bedrock
